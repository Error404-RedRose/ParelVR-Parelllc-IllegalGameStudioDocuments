Professional Statement

The company has engaged in unlawful activity involving five other virtual-reality
(VR) game providers, including legal disputes with VRChat and additional titles
currently under litigation with Parelllc.

All accusations, including alleged breaches of contract and other illegal
conduct against our organization, will be pursued through the appropriate
judicial process.

We will desist from promoting their products, citing ongoing violations of
business and contractual obligations.

The registered owner, aged 18, maintains a company that is falsely portrayed as
legally established. The entity is primarily a freelancer platform and is not
recognized as an authorized game studio.

The company has appropriated proprietary code from developers who expressly
prohibited public distribution, constituting illegal misuse.

Reports indicate the use of artificial-intelligence tools to generate complete
game content, with staff being removed impulsively without reference to
governing policies or legal agreements.

Pending confidential resolution, we will pursue its actions in court, joining
the five implicated parties in a consolidated case.

The owner admitted personnel departures during the development of ParelVR; our
assessment suggests these dismissals were unmerited and served to suppress
dissent rather than address legitimate policy concerns.

The matter falls under the jurisdiction of international and domestic laws
governing freelancing, online digital content creation, and employment.

Evidence links the organization's associates to the creation of illicit VRChat
modifications that compromise user accounts. Documentation from Discord archives
corroborates these allegations.

All claims are substantiated and will be presented to the relevant authorities
once legal proceedings commence.

EVIDENTIARY NOTE ON THIS STATEMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The statement uses strong language ("unlawful", "illegal", "appropriated"). In this
archive those words are the complainant's characterisations, not findings. The
factual record for the meeting and strike issue is in Document 12. The factual
record for the wider allegations is referred to in Documents 01, 04, 08 and 11 and
has not yet been reproduced in full.

The sentence "All claims are substantiated" is the author's assertion. It is not a
court finding. Counsel should verify each claim against the underlying records
before filing.
