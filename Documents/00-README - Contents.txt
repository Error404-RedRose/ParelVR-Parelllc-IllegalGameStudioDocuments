SCOPE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This archive documents alleged misconduct by the CEO of Parelllc / ParelVR, who
operates inside Discord as "Crispy", "CrispyAl_T" and "Gremix". The allegation is
systemic: the same treatment is reported by multiple organisations, studios,
contractors, investors, users and banned members. It is not a Twilight Studio
claim of ownership over Parelllc or ParelVR, and it is not a lawsuit.

The precipitating event is documented in Document 12: on 3 November 2026 the CEO
announced a same-day mandatory meeting and a strike penalty during the
complainant's pre-notified construction leave, then denied that a strike had been
given and threatened to remove the complainant's assets. Document 12 reproduces
the screenshots and identifies the contradictions. The later Gremix / eyeballs
exchange in Document 07 is part of the same chronology but is not the start of it.

The complainant is one individual using the names "eyeballs", "KittyFangs.exe" and
the Twilight Studio Head Administrator title. These names are consolidated in
Document 11.

Documents 09 and 10 are legal research indexes, not court orders.
