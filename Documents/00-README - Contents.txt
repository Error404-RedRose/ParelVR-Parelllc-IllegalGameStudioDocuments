SCOPE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This archive documents alleged misconduct by the CEO of Parelllc / ParelVR, who
operates inside Discord as "Gremix" and "Crispy". The allegation is systemic: the
same treatment is reported by multiple organisations, studios, contractors,
investors, users and banned members. It is not a Twilight Studio claim of ownership
over Parelllc or ParelVR, and it is not a lawsuit.

The Discord exchange between Gremix and eyeballs is a supplementary exhibit only
(Document 07). Documents 09 and 10 are legal research indexes, not court orders.
