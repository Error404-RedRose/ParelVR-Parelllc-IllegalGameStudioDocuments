WHAT THIS IS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This repository is a public notice file. It records allegations of abuse of power by
the CEO of Parelllc / ParelVR, who operates inside Discord under the names "Crispy",
"CrispyAl_T" and "Gremix".

It is NOT:
- a claim by Twilight Studio of ownership over Parelllc, ParelVR or their products;
- a lawsuit, injunction, court order or judgment;
- a statement that any allegation has been proven;
- a private dispute between Twilight Studio and Parelllc.

It IS:
- a public record of concerns raised by multiple organisations, studios, developers,
  freelancers, investors and users;
- an invitation for the CEO to respond and resolve the issues before publication;
- a place where affected parties can add their own documentation and open Issues.

The complainant is ONE participant. The names "eyeballs", "KittyFangs.exe" and the
Twilight Studio Head Administrator refer to the same individual. Many other
organisations and individuals report the same pattern and have not yet filed their
documents here. This archive will grow as those records are contributed.

WHAT THIS IS NOT ABOUT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This is not Twilight Studio versus Parelllc. Twilight Studio does not claim ownership
of Parelllc, ParelVR, their code, their worlds or their products. Twilight Studio is
one affected contractor among many. The subject is the conduct of the leadership and
whether affected parties can obtain resolution without litigation.

THE PRECIPITATING EVENT: 3 NOVEMBER 2026
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The dispute did not begin with the later public statements. It began when the CEO
imposed a same-day mandatory meeting and a strike penalty during the complainant's
pre-notified construction leave.

The sequence, documented in Document 12:

Date and time              Event
-------------------------  ------------------------------------------------------

                           for several days because of construction work with their

The exhibits show five things clearly:

1. The meeting was announced on the same day it was held. No schedule, agenda or
   notice period appears in the record.
2. The complainant had already given written notice of the construction work.
3. The complainant replied as soon as they returned from work, and the strike was
   communicated within minutes of that reply.
4. The CEO's own announcement explicitly threatened strikes, yet the CEO later said
   no strike had been given.
5. The CEO accepted the work explanation ("i said ur good") and then threatened to
   remove the complainant's assets because of the complainant's tone.

This is the strongest documented example of the pattern because the contradictions
are internal to the CEO's own written statements. The full transcript, the
time-stamp warning and the seven contradictions are in Document 12.

THE ALLEGED PATTERN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Policy imposed outside contract
   Unannounced "emergency meetings" enforced against freelancers and partner studios,
   with a 1/3 strike for missing them or not "saying hi", without a formal contract,
   notice or consent. The 3 November 2026 sequence is the primary example: a
   same-day mandatory meeting, a written strike threat, a pre-existing leave notice
   that was not applied, and a denial that any strike was given.

2. Bans without justification
   Members banned without discussion, a stated breach or an appeal, described as
   retaliation rather than discipline.

3. Fraud and illegally edited content
   Contributed work, credit, records and funds allegedly taken, edited or removed;
   contributors' names and existence erased while ownership is claimed.

4. Failure to cooperate and return
   The CEO allegedly fails to address concerns, cooperate with affected parties, or
   return and account for invested time, money, work and property.

5. Pressure on investors and partners
   Private funding expected to return a profit while partner studios, contractors and
   users are expelled for raising lawful concerns.

6. Security overreach
   A Linux server allegedly running hacking tools that give the owner full access to
   and control over connected PCs.

7. Denial and retaliation after objection
   When the complainant objected in writing and proposed a written contract, the CEO
   recharacterised the strike as "just a warning", said the complainant's contract
   "doesnt really count", and threatened to remove the complainant's assets from
   storage. The threat was conditioned on the complainant's tone, not on any policy
   violation.
