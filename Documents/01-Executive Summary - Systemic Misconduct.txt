DOCUMENT 01 - EXECUTIVE SUMMARY / PUBLIC NOTICE
PARELLLC / PARELLVR - SYSTEMIC MISCONDUCT
================================================================================

Status: Uncompiled public-notice record. Allegations only. Not a court order, not a
lawsuit, not a finding of fact, and not legal advice. See the repository README for
the full notice and right of reply.

WHAT THIS IS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This repository is a public notice file. It records allegations of abuse of power by
the CEO of Parelllc / ParelVR, who operates inside Discord under the names "Gremix"
and "Crispy".

It is NOT:
- a claim by Twilight Studio of ownership over Parelllc, ParelVR or their products;
- a lawsuit, injunction, court order or judgment;
- a statement that any allegation has been proven;
- a private dispute between Twilight Studio and Parelllc.

It IS:
- a public record of concerns raised by multiple organisations, studios, developers,
  freelancers, investors and users;
- an invitation for the CEO to respond and resolve the issues before publication;
- a place where affected parties can add their own documentation and open Issues.

The complainant (previously "eyeballs", of Twilight Studio) is ONE participant. Many
other organisations and individuals report the same pattern and have not yet filed
their documents here. This archive will grow as those records are contributed.

WHAT THIS IS NOT ABOUT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This is not Twilight Studio versus Parelllc. Twilight Studio does not claim ownership
of Parelllc, ParelVR, their code, their worlds or their products. Twilight Studio is
one affected contractor among many. The subject is the conduct of the leadership and
whether affected parties can obtain resolution without litigation.

THE ALLEGED PATTERN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Policy imposed outside contract
   Unannounced "emergency meetings" enforced against freelancers and partner studios,
   with a 1/3 strike for missing them or not "saying hi", without a formal contract,
   notice or consent.

2. Bans without justification
   Members banned without discussion, a stated breach or an appeal, described as
   retaliation rather than discipline.

3. Fraud and illegally edited content
   Contributed work, credit, records and funds allegedly taken, edited or removed;
   contributors' names and existence erased while ownership is claimed.

4. Failure to cooperate and return
   The CEO allegedly fails to address concerns, cooperate with affected parties, or
   return and account for invested time, money, work and property.

5. Pressure on investors and partners
   Private funding expected to return a profit while partner studios, contractors and
   users are expelled for raising lawful concerns.

6. Security overreach
   A Linux server allegedly running hacking tools that give the owner full access to
   and control over connected PCs.

WHO IS AFFECTED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Multiple VR and game organisations reporting the same experience with the same CEO.
- Freelancers and contractors banned after disputing policy.
- Contributors whose work, credit or compensation is alleged to have been removed.
- Investors and backers whose funds are alleged to be at risk.
- Members and users who hold sealed or DM documentation of comparable treatment.
- The complainant, who previously used the name "eyeballs".

The register in Document 11 records the parties and organisations identified so far,
including those whose documents are still being gathered. Legal names are only listed
where they are public or have been provided with consent and verification.

EVIDENCE BEING GATHERED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Direct-message (DM) records between affected members and the CEO.
- Publicly saved documents and posts concerning Parelllc.
- Sealed documents held by other members and organisations.
- Internal communications described as clear evidence of the alleged violations.
- Discord archive records.
- Screenshots referenced in the supplementary exchange (Document 07).
- Records from Steam publication, contracts, invoices and payment flows.

Because several records are sealed or privately held, this repository is an index and
a statement of allegations, not a complete evidence set.

NOTICE BEFORE PUBLICATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The CEO is asked to resolve the issues before the game is released on Steam. The
concerns are described as accumulating: unresolved bans, unpaid or uncredited work,
allegedly misappropriated code, illegal ports, and policies imposed without contract.
If they remain unresolved at publication, affected parties state they will cite them
in public reviews, platform reports and, where counsel advises, court filings.

This is a notice and an opportunity to respond. It is not a threat.

WHAT THE ARCHIVE ASKS FOR
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- A response from the CEO and Parelllc leadership to every affected party.
- Cooperation with all organisations and members, not only the complainant.
- Restoration or compensation for work, funds, credit and property taken.
- Withdrawal of policies imposed outside contract.
- Transparent, consistent moderation that leadership also follows.
- Preservation of all DM, public and sealed records pending resolution.
- No retaliation against anyone who contributes a report.
