READ THIS FIRST
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This register identifies the organisations and people referred to in the archive. It
is a working index, not a finding of fact and not a court order.

Naming rules (mandatory):
1. Do not publish a private individual's legal name unless it is already public or
   the person has given written consent.
2. Do not publish addresses, phone numbers, government identifiers, family members,
   employers or other personal data. This is doxxing and may be unlawful.
3. Use the platform username as the primary identifier until a legal name is
   verified and consented to.
4. If a person is a minor, do not name them at all.
5. Every entry must state the source of the information and whether it is verified.
6. Allegations must be labelled as allegations. Do not state guilt as fact.
7. A person who is named has a right of reply; record their response when received.

The register is intentionally incomplete. Other organisations and members have not
yet filed their documents. New entries should be added by pull request or by
opening an Issue using the template in .github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO ADD YOUR REPORT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Open the repository's Issues tab.
2. Choose "Abuse / Misconduct Report".
3. Complete the template fields.
4. Attach or link evidence. Do not upload private data you are not authorised to
   share.
5. State whether you consent to your username or legal name being published.
6. A maintainer will triage the report and, where appropriate, add it to this
   register.

Reports may be submitted anonymously. Anonymous reports can still be cited, but they
carry less weight in court than signed statements with preserved evidence.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT MAKES AN ENTRY USABLE IN COURT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- A signed or authenticated statement from the person who experienced the conduct.
- Original, unedited records with timestamps and metadata.
- Contracts, invoices, receipts and payment records.
- Consistent dates and names across documents.
- A clear chain of custody: who obtained the record, when, and how.
- Corroboration from independent witnesses or organisations.
- A documented response (or non-response) from the CEO.
- Evidence of loss: unpaid amounts, removed credit, deleted content, lost work.

Two weak entries do not become strong because they are repeated. Precision matters
more than volume.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PRIVACY AND SAFETY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Do not post threats, slurs or incitement.
- Do not post sexual content, medical records or identity documents.
- Do not name family members, minors or unrelated third parties.
- If you fear retaliation, report anonymously and keep originals in a safe place.
- If you are in immediate danger, contact local emergency services, not this
  repository.
