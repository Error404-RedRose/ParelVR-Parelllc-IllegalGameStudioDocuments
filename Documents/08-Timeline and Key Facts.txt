3. CORE FACTUAL ASSERTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.1 Policy imposed outside contract
- A staff attendance rule requiring unannounced "emergency meetings" is alleged to
  have been imposed without a formal contract, without notice and without the
  consent of partner studios.
- Members who missed the meetings, or did not "say hi", allegedly received a 1/3
  strike.
- The rule was pressed during the complainant's staff leave, which the other side
  allegedly had not read.
- The obligation is described as absolute despite there being no established
  contract.

3.2 Bans and retaliation
- Members are alleged to have been banned without discussion, a stated breach or an
  appeal.
- The complainant was allegedly banned after offering professional advice and
  disputing the policy; the complainant states no rules were broken during their
  time with the organisation.
- The complainant's main account was allegedly also banned from the Discord server.
- The complainant's hub world is stated to remain live on ParelVR.
- The complainant removed Parelllc from their server as a temporary punishment.

3.3 Fraud, credit and content
- Contributors are alleged to be credited and then removed from the project.
- The owner allegedly claims sole ownership of the software.
- ChilloutVR supplied a framework for private use only; it is allegedly presented
  publicly as the owner's own work.
- A game generated with AI (described as a Claude rewrite) is allegedly claimed as
  original.
- Multiple worlds are alleged to have been ported illegally to the owner's server.
- Proprietary code is alleged to have been appropriated from developers who
  prohibited public distribution.
- Content and records are alleged to have been illegally edited.
- Contributors allegedly worked without agreements or contracts.

3.4 Funding, governance and cooperation
- The registered owner is described as 18 years old with no legal background.
- The company is described as a freelancer platform falsely portrayed as a legally
  established, authorised game studio.
- Funding is alleged to come from a friend expecting a return on investment.
- Staff are alleged to be removed impulsively, without reference to policies or
  legal agreements, and to suppress dissent.
- The CEO is alleged to have failed to cooperate with affected organisations and
  members, and to have failed to return or account for invested time and money.

3.5 Security
- The server is stated to run Linux and to use hacking tools that provide the
  owner full access to and control over connected PCs.

3.6 Legal exposure asserted
- Alleged unlawful activity involving five other VR game providers, including
  VRChat and additional titles under litigation with Parelllc.
- Alleged breaches of contract and other illegal conduct.
- Alleged illicit VRChat modifications that compromise user accounts, with Discord
  archives cited as corroboration.
- Asserted jurisdiction: international and domestic law governing freelancing,
  online digital content creation and employment.
- Stated intent to consolidate the five implicated parties into one case.

The statutory authorities that may apply are set out in Documents 09 (United
States) and 10 (international and common law). No court order exists.

5. REQUESTED REMEDIES (FROM DOCUMENT 06)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

- Cease unlawful dismissal of developers without proper compensation.

  implemented, protecting the interests of investors and developers.
