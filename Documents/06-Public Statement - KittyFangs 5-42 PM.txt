This system exemplifies a game development project that fails to expand and
instead harms all participants. Leadership exploits personal connections and
financial resources, relying on a friend's funding rather than its own capital.
The friend expects a return on investment, yet each day brings the project
closer to termination due to the leader's abusive behavior and arbitrary bans,
even for minor language infractions. Such actions erase years of developers'
work and undermine organizational collaboration, contrary to best practices for
sustainable growth.

The leader now depends on AI-generated code because he refuses to integrate
other organizations or new team members, while continuing to misuse his friend's
money and time. This dynamic strains the friend's trust, as competent developers
are expelled for voicing dissent. The service host has noted these concerns, and
the financial flow to the leader appears questionable, resembling a mismanaged
corporate structure.

It is imperative that the project's leadership:

  - Acknowledge and credit all contributors appropriately.
  - Cease unlawful dismissal of developers without proper compensation.
  - Reallocate funds responsibly, honoring the financial contributions made by
    collaborators.
  - Establish transparent, consistent moderation and policy guidelines that the
    leader also adheres to.
  - Address contractual obligations and rectify any misappropriation of work or
    data.
  - Consider discontinuing the project if these corrective measures are not
    implemented, thereby protecting the interests of investors and developers.

Failure to address these issues will likely provoke strong reactions from the
server host, the funding friend, and external observers, potentially leading to
legal ramifications and loss of credibility for the organization.
