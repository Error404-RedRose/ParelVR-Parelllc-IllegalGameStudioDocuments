## Public notice, not a lawsuit

This repository exists to give the CEO of Parelllc / ParelVR a documented opportunity to
respond and resolve the issues **before the game is published on Steam**.

It is:

- a public record of allegations raised by **many organisations and individuals**, not one
  studio;
- an index of documents being gathered for potential court use;
- an open reporting point where affected parties can add their accounts through **Issues**;
- a request for cooperation, credit, payment and the withdrawal of policies imposed
  outside contract.

It is **not**:

- a lawsuit, injunction, court order, judgment or regulatory decision;
- a claim that Twilight Studio owns Parelllc, ParelVR or their products;
- a statement that any allegation has been proven;
- a private dispute between Twilight Studio and Parelllc;
- legal advice.

> **Twilight Studio is one participant.** The complainant (previously **"eyeballs"**) is one
> affected contractor among many. Other organisations and members have not yet filed their
> documents here; this archive will grow as those records are contributed. See
> [Document 11](Documents/11-Involved%20Parties%20and%20Case%20Register.txt).

## Scope: the alleged systemic pattern

| # | Alleged pattern | Description |
|---|-----------------|-------------|
| 1 | **Policy imposed outside contract** | Unannounced "emergency meetings" enforced against freelancers and partner studios, with a **1/3 strike** for missing them or not "saying hi" — without a formal contract, notice or consent. |
| 2 | **Bans without justification** | Members banned without discussion, a stated breach or an appeal, described as retaliation rather than discipline. |
| 3 | **Fraud and illegally edited content** | Contributed work, credit, records and funds allegedly taken, edited or removed; contributors' names and existence erased while ownership is claimed. |
| 4 | **Failure to cooperate and return** | The CEO allegedly fails to address concerns, cooperate with affected parties, or return and account for invested time, money, work and property. |
| 5 | **Pressure on investors and partners** | Private funding expected to return a profit while partner studios, contractors and users are expelled for raising lawful concerns. |
| 6 | **Security overreach** | A Linux server allegedly running hacking tools that give the owner full access to and control over connected PCs. |

## What would need to be proven

| Element | Why it matters | Evidence needed |
|---------|----------------|-----------------|
| **Employment or worker status** | Most wage, retaliation and discrimination statutes protect employees or workers, not independent contractors. | Contracts, control indicators, payment structure, integration into the business. |
| **A protected activity** | Retaliation claims need a complaint, report or refusal that the law protects. | The message, email or meeting record where the concern was raised. |
| **Causation and timing** | The ban or penalty must be linked to the protected activity. | Sequence of events, decision-maker statements, comparator treatment. |
| **A contract or promise** | Unpaid-work claims need an agreement, promise or unjust enrichment. | SOW, invoice, DM agreement, payment history. |
| **Ownership or licence terms** | IP claims turn on who owned the work and under what licence. | Contracts, EULAs, contribution records, git history, asset licences. |
| **Unauthorised access** | Computer-access claims need proof of access beyond authorisation. | Server logs, configuration, forensic image, access records. |
| **Loss and damages** | Courts need a measurable harm. | Unpaid amounts, lost revenue, removed credit, deleted content. |
| **Publication fault** | If the repository is sued for defamation, fault and truth/opinion defences matter. | Sourced documents, right-of-reply records, corrections log. |

The full evidence checklist is at
[Document 09, line 335](Documents/09-Legal%20Framework%20-%20United%20States.txt#L335).

## Report abuse via the Issues tab

The repository has a structured **Abuse / Misconduct Report** form in the Issues tab. It is
defined in [`.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/abuse-report.md`](.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/abuse-report.md).

**How to submit**

1. Open the **Issues** tab.
2. Choose **Abuse / Misconduct Report**.
3. Complete the fields: organisation, username, role, jurisdiction, dates, allegations,
   evidence held, loss claimed and relief sought.
4. State whether you consent to your username or legal name being published.
5. Attach or link evidence **only if you are authorised to share it**.

**Do not post**

- addresses, phone numbers, government IDs, financial account numbers or identity documents;
- medical records, sexual content, or data about family members or minors;
- private DM logs without the consent of every participant;
- threats, slurs or incitement.

**Anonymous reports** are accepted. They carry less weight in court than signed statements
with preserved originals, but they can still be indexed. The register and case template are
at [Document 11, line 100](Documents/11-Involved%20Parties%20and%20Case%20Register.txt#L100).

## Publication safety and right of reply

- **Allegations, not findings.** Every claim is an unverified allegation made by the
  publishing party or by third parties quoted here.
- **Presumption of innocence.** Parelllc, ParelVR, the CEO (Gremix / Crispy) and every other
  named or unnamed person may respond, correct and provide context.
- **No court order.** No court, regulator or platform has ruled on these matters as of the
  archive date, unless separately documented.
- **Not legal advice.** Obtain independent counsel before relying on this material.
- **Corrections are welcome.** Open an Issue or pull request with the correction and its
  source. Corrections will be recorded, not silently deleted.
- **No licence** is granted for third-party reuse of personal images or private
  communications. The material is published for documentation and accountability.
- **Defamation risk is real.** Under *New York Times v. Sullivan*, 376 U.S. 254 (1964), and
  *Milkovich v. Lorain Journal*, 497 U.S. 1 (1990), the way allegations are framed matters.
  Keep statements sourced, dated and labelled as allegations or opinion.
