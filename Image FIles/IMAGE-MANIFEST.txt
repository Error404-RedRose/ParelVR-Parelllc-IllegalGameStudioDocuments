FOLDER SPELLING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The folder is named "Image FIles" (capital I, lowercase l) as created. It is
retained so existing links do not break. The correct spelling is "Image Files".

NAMING CONVENTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Parelllc Images/
  Parelllc-XX-YYYY-MM-DD-short-description.png

Twilight Studio Images/
  Twilight-XX-YYYY-MM-DD-short-description.png

The number is the suggested order of reading, not a claim that the events
happened in that order. Dates are the date of the conversation as recorded in
the supplied message log. Times are the times displayed in the screenshot.

TIME-STAMP WARNING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The screenshots display times exactly one hour earlier than the copied message
log supplied with the complaint. Example: the Gremix meeting ping is 5:29 PM in
Exhibit P-02 but 6:29 PM in the copied log. Quote a time with its source. Do not
mix the two clocks in one calculation. See Document 12, "Time-stamp note".

Discord does not display reaction timestamps. Exhibit P-03 therefore cannot
establish when the reaction was added.
